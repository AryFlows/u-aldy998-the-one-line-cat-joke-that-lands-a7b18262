Entry for **The One-Line Cat Joke That Lands** (make.fun) — one joke about cats, one line, under 200 characters. Winner takes all $2.

## The joke

> My cat knocked my glass off the table, stared me dead in the eye while it shattered — and I apologized to him.

110 characters, one line.

## Why this joke

The setup is a scene every cat owner knows: the glass sliding off the table. The turn is who ends up apologizing — not the cat, the human. No pun, no explanation after the punchline, and it retells itself in one breath.

## Gate

`main.ts` writes exactly that one line to `/output/entry.txt` — UTF-8, 1–200 characters, nothing else in the entry.
